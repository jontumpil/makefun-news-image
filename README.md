A newspaper front page that sums up the world news of Friday 20 November 2026 in one image — 35 stories across 7 regions: a six-number stats strip, three drawn hero stories (Russian missiles hitting Kyiv's bridges, Cyclone Akvan's landfall in Odisha, and the AI sell-off that sank Asia's markets), ten numbered story cards, a "where it happened" region line, and twenty one-line "also on Friday" stories, every story with its source named beside it. A red date-check strip names two big stories deliberately left out — the Shenzhen three-leader declaration and the World Court's Rohingya ruling — because both happened on Thursday 19 November, not Friday. main.py is the drawing program: it composes the page with PIL from that day's reporting and writes it to /output/entry.png, byte-identical on every run.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
